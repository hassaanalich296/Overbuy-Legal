Overbuy Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026. Applies to the current Overbuy iOS app, developed by Hassaan Chaudhry.

Information on your device

Overbuy helps you pause before opening shopping apps. It stores your chosen app tokens, protection preferences, onboarding progress, wallet health and wallet history locally on your device. App tokens are the opaque selections supplied by Apple's Screen Time APIs; Overbuy does not translate them into a list of app identities for a server.

Optional onboarding answers personalize setup text. They are stored locally while setup is in progress and removed when you complete or defer setup. You can skip either question.

The app and its Screen Time extensions can store local records of supported shield interactions and Device Activity callbacks, including identifiers, timestamps and configuration information. These records are used for local diagnostics and, only where validated and enabled, behavior-based features. The current build does not use these records to change wallet health automatically. Missing activity data is not treated as shopping behavior.

Overbuy does not read shopping receipts, payment-card details, shopping purchases, bank accounts or actual spending. It does not require you to log purchases and does not calculate actual savings from app activity.

What is shared

Overbuy has no developer-operated account server, advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. The app does not upload your selected apps, onboarding answers, wallet or activity records to the developer, sell them, or share them for advertising. Local records are shared between Overbuy and its own extensions where needed for Screen Time functionality.

Apple provides Screen Time authorization, restriction services and App Store purchases. To offer and verify subscriptions, Overbuy receives product information and verified transaction information from StoreKit, such as the subscription product, transaction identifier, renewal status and expiration. Apple processes payment and account information under Apple's privacy policy. Overbuy does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may also process diagnostics according to your device settings.

Overbuy does not provide its own cloud synchronization. iOS device backups and restores may include app data depending on your Apple settings; these are managed by Apple, not an Overbuy server.

Retention and your controls

Wallet history, selections, preferences and locally recorded diagnostic events can remain on your device until removed. Optional onboarding answers have the shorter retention described above. No server-side copy of these app records is held by Overbuy.

You can disable protection in Overbuy and manage its Screen Time permission through iOS Settings. Revoking permission does not delete your wallet history. To remove app data, delete Overbuy from your device; manage existing device backups separately through Apple's settings. Disable protection before removal when possible. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription: manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings.

This policy website and support

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends normal web-request information to GitHub, which handles that information under the GitHub privacy statement. The policy page adds no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

For questions about this policy, open an issue in Overbuy's public policy and support repository. GitHub issues are public. Do not post app tokens, shopping activity, transaction details or other sensitive information there. Information you voluntarily submit to GitHub is separate from the app's local records and is handled under GitHub's policies.

Changes

This page will be updated if Overbuy's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
